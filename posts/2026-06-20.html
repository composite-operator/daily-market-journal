Regime Map

The market is being pulled between two liquidity messages. The first is a short-term easing impulse from lower oil and headline de-escalation. The second is a tightening impulse from the Fed, the dollar, and inflation data risk. The update treats the second force as more durable unless the dollar fails and volatility compresses.

Energy is not a solved bearish story. The paper price of oil can fall while the physical system remains undersupplied. Strait flows improved from crisis levels, but they were still far below normal in the update, so the energy relief trade may be a reprieve rather than a regime change.

The AI and semiconductor sleeve is still the support beam under the headline indices. That is strength while the squeeze continues, but fragility if memory, data-center capex, or semiconductor leadership breaks. A broad bull case needs the AI trade to broaden into utilities, industrial infrastructure, power, and grid buildout without the dollar and rates undermining it.

  • A bull-market continuation needs leadership broadening into power, grid, industrials, and cash-flow beneficiaries, not only more chip multiple expansion.
  • Boxes are reaction zones, not permission slips. Require divergence repair, macro confirmation, and actual reaction.
