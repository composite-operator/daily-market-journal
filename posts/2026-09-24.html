Capital Flow Channels

Reuters reported financing concerns around Oracle's New Mexico project after its force-majeure notice to a Blue Owl development unit. Blue Owl said financial commitments were unchanged. This is evidence of project-execution and financing risk, not confirmation of a default or disappearing AI demand.

Mechanism: delayed power delivery can defer revenue while financing costs continue. Higher rates make that timing mismatch more expensive. META's product-led optimism and ORCL's infrastructure concern can therefore coexist: demand, monetization, construction, and funding are separate parts of the AI chain. A rally in one does not settle the economics of the others.

Regime test: a durable improvement needs rates and the dollar to ease without a new growth or credit shock. Oil relief would help importers and margins, but weaker oil caused by demand destruction would carry a different meaning. Support in TLT, real estate, dividend shares, and small caps is a cross-market hypothesis to test, not proof that rescue policy is imminent.

Research Rules

  1. Timestamp the forecast, not just the post. Evaluate the morning warning and closing revision separately. Freeze each trigger and horizon before scoring subsequent returns.
  2. Distinguish flat indexes from repaired breadth. Compare index returns with equal-weight performance, participation, and the rate trend over the following one and five sessions.
  3. Separate demand from project finance. Test AI revenue expectations against power availability, completion dates, funding terms, and cash-generation timing rather than treating all AI names as one trade.
  4. Keep losses in the record. A breached stop remains a failed setup even if price rebounds. A replacement entry must have a new identifier and cannot inherit a fictitious winning fill.
  5. Test support clusters without assuming intervention. Require actual bond and breadth improvement. Record policy announcements only when verified, not when a chart makes a rescue seem desirable.
  6. Bound reclaim attempts and correlated exposure. Use fixed total loss budgets, execution costs, and aggregate crypto or duration exposure when comparing a reclaim strategy with one-entry alternatives.
