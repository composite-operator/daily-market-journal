Macro Flow Tape

Treasury intervention: The buyback was economically small relative to the Treasury market, but symbolically large. It revealed concern about the long end and reminded traders that more forceful tools could follow. The distinction matters: a positioning squeeze can lower yields for a session; durable liquidity expansion requires the Federal Reserve balance sheet or a sustained financing regime change.

Rates and duration: The primary thesis still expects the long end to rebuild and move higher after the intervention shock. A quick yield reversal would pressure semiconductors, discretionary stocks, housing, and rate-sensitive health care again. Persistent relief would instead validate the new SPY, IWM, housing, and consumer boxes.

Dollar and emerging markets: The dollar broke lower in an impulsive move but has not completed a clean bottoming process. A continued dollar decline supports China, Brazil, metals, and crypto. A fast dollar recovery would reveal the move as a positioning trap.

Crypto: Bitcoin's squeeze toward 72,000 rewarded the existing 62,000 long. Ethereum and Solana also repaired, with later pullback controls discussed near 1,822 and 70.52 respectively. Those are second-chance concepts, not permission to chase the headline.

Energy and metals: Oil remains a geopolitical and inflation hedge, but its next advance can also limit index upside by rebuilding yield pressure. Gold and silver rose with the weaker dollar, yet copper lagged and the gold-silver ratio refused to confirm. That divergence argues for waiting for pullbacks instead of extrapolating a new straight-line metals regime.
