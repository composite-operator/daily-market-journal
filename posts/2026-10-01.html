Research Rules

  • Separate a rates reprieve from a regime reversal. Require a defended bond low and multi-session follow-through before assuming the financing constraint is removed.
  • Count shared macro risk, not just ticker count. Several housing, utility, dividend, and small-cap positions can depend on the same decline in yields. Common exposure is not identical payoff or perfect correlation.
  • Cancel the setup when its structure expires. A price running away from a pending entry can invalidate that entry without invalidating the broader bullish thesis.
  • Do not resurrect stopped trades with quote formulas. A new mark above an old stop cannot erase an earlier stop event or establish a fresh execution.
  • Treat screening output as a candidate generator. Verify the instrument, wave structure, entry direction, stop, and reward before promoting an alert.
  • Test the jobs reaction across channels. Lower yields with stable credit and improving breadth differ from lower yields accompanied by earnings stress and wider credit spreads.
