Regime Map

The tape is trying to price a rapid geopolitical de-escalation: energy and inflation-sensitive trades weakened while food, real estate, software, and other disinflation beneficiaries caught bids. That rotation is not yet trusted. Oil structure still allows another advance, the dollar is attempting to break higher, and long-end rates have not delivered the easing required for a durable small-cap or duration rally.

If semiconductors extend lower while the dollar rises and the 20-year bond fails, rotation into software or mega-caps is unlikely to absorb the selling.

Regime Rules Learned

  • A rotation is not bullish if the replacement leaders cannot keep the capitalization-weighted indexes afloat.
  • Use SMH and EWY as confirmation gates for QQQ support when AI concentration dominates index behavior.
  • Disinflation trades require confirmation from oil, the dollar, and long rates; headline de-escalation alone is insufficient.
  • A support box is a position-sizing process. Start small when the larger impulse may still be extending and add only after breadth and divergence improve.
  • When the dollar rises while the bond market fails to ease, downgrade metals, small caps, real estate, and speculative duration simultaneously.
  • Conditional stop changes should not be applied before their price trigger occurs.
