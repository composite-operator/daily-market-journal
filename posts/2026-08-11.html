Research Rules Learned

  • Do not predict an economic release. Define the reaction branches in advance and classify the regime from the joint price response.
  • Trade each chart on its own unless a direct composition relationship exists. Commodity strength does not prove industrial or economic strength.
  • Use sector components to confirm an ETF. SMH requires agreement from AMD, NVDA, and the broader semiconductor basket.
  • Separate a speculative reaction level from an official setup. The IWM 297 area is research context, not a ledger mutation.
  • When a verbal cancellation conflicts with the imported board, preserve both states and elevate the conflict to Needs Review.
  • Do not treat a strong rebound as proof of a durable low when the higher-timeframe cycle and divergence remain unresolved.
