Regime Map

The main regime tension is failed-cycle risk versus crypto/resource reflation. The Dow failed a cycle structure and broke a key low. That tilts the immediate edge toward bears, especially if the next session confirms with a direct lower break. But the market has not lost every risk channel: crypto is trying to turn, oil is firm, and selected copper/resource structures still have constructive long-term maps.

The dollar is the gate. If DXY fails support, metals, crypto, commodities, China/EM, and high beta can get another relief window. If DXY squeezes while yen stays weak and oil holds up, liquidity tightens across the same assets at once. That is why this read should not be reduced to "bullish" or "bearish." It is a fork where confirmation matters more than narrative.

Rates did not fully relax after a softer inflation print. That matters. If oil keeps pressing higher and policy remains less forgiving, rate relief may be tactical rather than durable. A market trying to rally on lower-rate hopes can still be vulnerable if long-end yields, oil, or the dollar reassert pressure.
