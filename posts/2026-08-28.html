Macro Flow Tape

Policy transmission: The post-event reaction says the market heard a less forgiving inflation response. That observation is stronger than any single policy forecast. Higher short rates, a stronger dollar, and weaker metals show that financial conditions tightened through prices even before any future decision is known.

Cross-market non-confirmation: IYR and KRE did not fall enough to confirm the most aggressive tightening narrative. That is useful contrary evidence. A real regime shift should propagate through real estate, regional banks, small caps, the dollar, and the rates complex rather than remain concentrated in metals and index futures.

Metals and the event pivot: The reversal in gold, silver, and miners established a usable risk pivot. It did not prove a completed long-cycle top. New boxes create conditional opportunities only after daily and weekly divergence repair. A policy follow-through that keeps the dollar rising would extend the correction; policy talk without action could reverse the pivot quickly.

Agriculture and food inflation: Wheat and soybeans are accelerating, but the larger food-supply thesis remains a hypothesis. Weather damage, crop failures, inventory stress, and hoarding must be verified independently. Price strength alone can identify a tradeable flow, but it cannot prove a future food crisis.

Concentration and valuation: Historical comparisons with railroads, the Nifty Fifty, Japan, and the dot-com era are analogies, not proof that AI leadership must collapse. Their value is mechanical: concentrated capital can keep compounding until rates, earnings, or liquidity break the reflexive loop. The correct test is whether semiconductor weakness spreads into software, credit, breadth, and the major indexes.

Energy and geopolitics: Contradictory ceasefire and shipping headlines are not reliable evidence. Use oil, freight, insurance, and inflation expectations as observable transmission channels. A durable oil rise alongside a stronger dollar and higher rates would be the most restrictive combination.
