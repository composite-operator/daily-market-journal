June 13 is a weekend regime-map day, not a clean chase day. Cross-Market Synthesis

The strongest theme is that the market may be pricing easier financial conditions before the evidence has arrived. Financials, real estate, dividend/value exposure, and some rate-sensitive areas are behaving as if Fed language, bank regulation, or future easing will loosen conditions. That creates upside risk into the Fed event, especially for bears, but it also raises the risk of a later trap if the enthusiasm burns off into hidden bearish divergence.

The bearish case is not simply "war bad." The deeper macro argument is demand destruction and global growth scare. Oil weakness may be misread as geopolitical relief when it may also reflect slowing demand, rationing, China stress, and consumer pressure. Copper, XME, uranium, gold, silver, China, emerging markets, and crypto are not yet aligned for a broad three-of-three risk impulse.

The bullish case is a final fifth-wave or future blowoff scenario. It requires a weaker dollar, China and emerging markets to turn up, financials to resolve constructively, and big-cap tech to stop bleeding. Surprise easing, dovish Fed communication, reduced bank constraints, or an explicit liquidity pivot would be the wild-card accelerant. Without that, the read expects bounces to remain connector waves or dead-cat rallies in many high-beta names.
