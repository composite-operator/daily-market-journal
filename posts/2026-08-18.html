Risk Notes

Do not confuse a red session with an automatic short. The short-cycle structure can still produce abrupt squeezes and relief rallies. The preferred process is to use stated levels, wait for divergence and breadth confirmation, and size risk for the possibility that a corrective move extends.

For existing positions, preserve the difference between an original entry and a later second-chance opportunity. Newer verbal buy-backs should be treated as review items unless the level and invalidation are both clearly stated.

Bottom Line

Rates, oil, the dollar, and semiconductor leadership form the main decision stack. A decisive extension lower would validate a more defensive regime; a failed breakdown with improving breadth would favor a tradable relief phase.
