Macro Flow Tape

Rates and housing: Long-end yields are the central pressure channel. A 30-year yield at its highest area since 2007 raises the discount rate for growth stocks while weak housing starts show the real-economy cost. Equity relief is less durable if yields keep rising.

Dollar, yen, and global liquidity: The dollar remains unresolved but is running out of time to choose a direction. USDJPY near 160.90 is an important control: a sustained break higher would increase intervention and funding stress. Dollar strength would pressure emerging markets, crypto, metals, and high beta together; dollar failure would support hard assets but could revive inflation pressure.

Energy and geopolitics: Oil remains constructive from the prior 80 area. A push through 94 would keep 104-108 in view. That is positive for energy leadership, but it also acts as an inflation tax and can keep long rates elevated.

Metals: Gold below 4,300 leaves a move toward 4,000 open. Silver below 61 preserves the weaker structure. The gold-silver ratio at 66 is the control: strength above it favors defensive gold leadership and can point toward 82-84.

Crypto: Bitcoin remains a trendline decision rather than a clean new setup. The existing long originated near 62,000. The decisive continuation and failure points were shown as chart labels rather than spoken numbers, so they remain visual review items.
