Research Rules

  1. Do not equate relief with repair. Require breadth, long-rate relief, contained volatility, and stable oil before promoting a bounce to a regime change.
  2. Separate the AI capacity cycle from the hardware trade. Physical investment can remain necessary while crowded equity leadership corrects.
  3. Use the joint transmission. Oil, rates, dollar, yen, volatility, credit, and small-cap breadth are more informative together than any one chart.
  4. Retire invalidated geometry. A new deeper correction is not the old box reactivated. Record a new level only when the instrument and number are explicit.
  5. Keep temporal records straight. A post-session export comparison is not a real-time change log; a spoken fill is not the same as an active Ledger row.
  6. Write both branches. If software strength broadens while oil, yields, and VIX fall, weaken the defensive thesis. If semis and small caps extend lower as those pressure gauges rise, strengthen it.

Bottom Line

September 14 did not deliver a single-direction verdict. The tape rotated toward software while semiconductor and small-cap weakness, oil supply risk, and long rates kept the broader structure fragile. An oversold rebound can still be tradable. It becomes durable repair only with broader participation and relief in oil, yields, and volatility after the Fed. If those pressure gauges accelerate together and leadership narrows further, the defensive branch gains evidence. Several sheet rows need reconciliation before their levels can be treated as current setups.
