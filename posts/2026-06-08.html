Trade Management Rules

  • Separate watchlists from active trades. Watchlist rows are candidates. Active trades should remain in their own section until manually removed or archived.
  • Buy box means target entry. A committed buy box is the automatic trigger area on the board; do not create a separate concept unless the entry is explicitly a second-chance entry.
  • Second-chance entries overwrite only when better defined. If a new buyback level supersedes an older missed-entry level, keep the newest explicit level and preserve the original entry as historical context.
  • Do not loosen stops after a failed box reaction. Several filled boxes now need immediate extension. Failure should move the setup to review or closed, not to a wider-risk thesis.
  • Equity prices in snapshots reflect the 4pm closing price. Futures and crypto prices may reflect the true price at the time of the snapshot.

Regime Rules Learned

  • A multi-sector selloff with VIX holding firm is different from a routine bullish pullback. It deserves regime-level caution.
  • Dollar strength plus yen weakness can turn normal equity weakness into a liquidity event.
  • Industrial, financial, material, and housing breakdowns matter more when semis and mega-cap tech stop masking breadth.
  • Box entries are useful only when reaction appears quickly. A dead reaction inside a box is information, not patience by itself.
  • Rate-sensitive growth groups can fail even if they look cheap on the chart, especially when the dollar and volatility are pressing higher.
