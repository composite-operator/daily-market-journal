Cross-Market Synthesis

The core regime test is dollar and yen first, headlines second. If the dollar holds its backtest and starts higher while the yen strengthens under BOJ pressure, risk assets likely lose the pump and the move can become a broad double-correction / flat across world markets.

The Fed window is the other hinge. A hawkish Fed keeps the defensive path alive: higher dollar, higher long rates, volatility bid, metals pressure, and weaker high-beta. A dovish surprise or backdoor liquidity cue could instead force the bearish divergence to erase and allow a blowoff-style continuation.

Energy is not being treated as a simple peace-deal unwind. The risk read is that lower crude may eventually come from demand destruction and growth fear, while short-term geopolitics can still whipsaw prices upward. That creates a strange setup: energy equities may be attractive on deep double corrections, but the path there can be violent.

Metals remain tactically dangerous. The gold/silver ratio is in a key three-wave area; if it pushes toward 80-82 while silver and gold reach their lower boxes, that becomes the cleaner long setup. Until then, the metals can still flush even if the longer-term view stays constructive.
