Macro Flow Tape

Policy and rates: Resilient jobless claims and multiple officials discussing tighter policy kept long yields elevated. Jackson Hole matters less as a single speech than as a test of whether inflation, Treasury financing, and the long end can coexist with the market's assumption of eventual easing. A break higher in long yields is the cleanest bearish transmission channel into small caps and long-duration growth.

Dollar, oil, and the real move: The dollar, oil, and rates are the three instruments most likely to separate a durable index move from intraday noise. A lower dollar can extend metals, crypto, and equities; a decisive dollar bottom would tighten global liquidity. Oil regained risk premium as the prior de-escalation narrative weakened. A simultaneous rise in oil and yields would pressure both margins and valuation.

Volatility and positioning: VIX and UVXY showed very little protection into the policy event while dip buyers displayed aggressive chase behavior. That creates asymmetric event risk. A benign speech can force remaining bears to capitulate above the highs, but a hawkish shock can produce a faster selloff because the long side is crowded and underhedged.

AI and software: Nvidia reignited the AI trade, but the more interesting flow was the software rotation. ServiceNow, Salesforce, Okta, cybersecurity, and IGV showed that capital is searching for earnings-backed beneficiaries beyond hardware. This is selective leadership: buying the software group indiscriminately remains dangerous because winners and structurally impaired names can diverge sharply.

Commodities and inflation: Gold and silver retain upside paths if the dollar weakens, but existing exposure should be distinguished from fresh chasing into a binary event. Wheat, soybeans, uranium, energy, and natural gas continue to supply an inflation-sensitive leadership sleeve. If those markets strengthen while rates rise, the market is signaling reflation rather than clean easing.

Market cycle: Many instruments are correcting at higher degrees and trading in middle areas. This is why a few clean trends attract disproportionate capital while most setups remain slow and erratic. The correct response is lower turnover, patience for defined boxes, and preference for trades aligned with the larger cycle.
