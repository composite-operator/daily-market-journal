Research Rules

  1. Separate geometry from timing. A harmonic or wave structure defines an area; divergence, breadth, and momentum decide whether the area is ready.
  2. Do not confuse patience with validity. Waiting helps only while the invalidation remains intact and the original horizon still applies.
  3. Classify the joint move. Oil, the dollar, long rates, the yen, volatility, and breadth must be read together.
  4. Keep relief and regime separate. A short rebound can occur inside a larger liquidity contraction.
  5. Record unresolved sheet conflicts. Do not silently convert a spoken status change into a tracking list mutation when the price or identity is incomplete.
  6. Require cross-sector confirmation. Energy strength alone does not prove broad-market health.
  7. Treat experimental screening as discovery. AI rankings can surface candidates, but they do not replace structure, liquidity, entry, stop, and independent confirmation.

Bottom Line

The market can still produce a tactical relief bounce, but the burden of proof moved higher. Oil above 107, rising long rates, firm volatility, and weakening growth leadership describe tightening financial conditions, not clean risk expansion. Relief requires cooler inflation, lower yields, contained volatility, and broader participation. Until those gates improve, energy and selected hard assets can outperform while the broader tape remains defensive.
