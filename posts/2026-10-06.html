Today's Operating Read

October 6: record-level large-cap indices masked weaker small-cap participation. Falling yields gave valuation relief, but the leadership signal was still concentrated in artificial-intelligence infrastructure, semiconductors, and the power complex.

The day's clearest fundamental catalyst was the Google-Constellation agreement. It calls for 890 MW of new nuclear capacity across 11 units, a 20-year power purchase agreement, a separate 15-year agreement covering 2,700 MW, and more than $4.3 billion of investment. That converts the AI-power theme from a narrative into contracted grid demand. Constellation announcement.

The rate backdrop eased but did not resolve. Real yields also declined across the 5- through 30-year points. Treasury nominal curve; Treasury real curve.

Working interpretation: the tape is broadening through power rather than through general risk appetite. The constructive branch needs long yields to remain contained and small caps to hold their structural lows. A renewed yield advance would expose the difference between durable contracted demand and late-cycle momentum chasing.

Research Rules

  • RR-05: Remove consumed plans. Once a box fills or is cancelled, it leaves the live watchlist.
  • RR-06: Do not manufacture exits. A Stopped label without an exit date and price is not enough to calculate realized performance.
