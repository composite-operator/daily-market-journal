Today's Operating Read

Macro Flow Tape

Dollar and metals: The dollar is at a decision point. Holding roughly above 99.2 leaves a counter-rally and renewed pressure on metals, crypto, and high beta viable. A sustained dollar failure would instead support a larger metals extension. Gold and silver therefore remain a cross-asset confirmation test rather than an isolated momentum trade.

Rates and liquidity: Long rates are rising out of a short consolidation. That is the principal risk to duration, housing-sensitive assets, solar, dividend proxies, and expensive growth. A reversal in the long end would revive the opposite transmission: bonds, housing, rate-sensitive equities, and selected health-care or income sleeves could all benefit together.

Energy, materials, and freight: Energy and small-cap energy still show constructive breadth, and commodities may be preparing to resume higher. Uranium completed its immediate reaction, so the next valid opportunity requires a new controlled pullback. Freight, shipping, and automation remain research sleeves; wait for retracements rather than chase extended charts.

Crypto and volatility: Bitcoin remains constructive only while its recent floor holds. A decisive break there, combined with a rising VIX and stronger dollar, would convert the current risk-on extension into a liquidity-warning signal.
