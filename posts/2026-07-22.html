Regime Map

The market is no longer trading like a clean risk-on tape. Breadth, software, semiconductors, mega-cap reactions, higher rates, and dollar strength are all pressuring the same side of the boat. The constructive side is still concentrated in energy, selected commodity exposure, and carefully defined inflation-linked trades.

Equities can still squeeze if the dollar and volatility fail to confirm. The burden of proof has moved to the bulls: support has to hold, volatility has to compress, and leadership cannot keep failing on earnings.

Setup Mechanics

Do not chase acceleration into lower boxes. Several tech and index structures can gap or waterfall into support. The better trade is controlled movement into a defined box, not a panic fill where the wave count is still expanding.

Separate hedge entries from long-term longs. SPXS and UVIX are tactical defensive instruments. Energy, copper, and select inflation-linked structures are different: they are trend-continuation or second-chance entries that need their own invalidation rules.

Respect earnings proximity. BE and NEM both illustrate the same rule: a technically valid box becomes riskier when the next candle is an earnings event.
