Research Rules

  • RR-01 / Rebound test: over the next three sessions, record SPY closes relative to 757 and 764.26, then inspect whether the first pullback holds. A brief intraday cross is weaker evidence than a retained reclaim.
  • RR-02 / Breadth test: compare SMH with IWM and the Dow over five sessions. A chip rally with persistent weakness elsewhere remains narrow; do not relabel it broad risk-on.
  • RR-03 / Timeframe discipline: score the daily rebound and the weekly correction as separate hypotheses. One can succeed while the other remains unfinished.
  • RR-04 / Record integrity: keep dated lots separate. Never infer a fill, closure, initial stop, or R multiple from current board membership alone.
  • RR-05 / Independent setups: harmonic entries require their own pattern, invalidation, and timeframe. Do not assign them the stops of a different wave-based plan.
  • RR-06 / Unscored is not successful: no outcome is claimed for these new tests. Log the observation date and price series before later scoring.
