Regime Map

The market is not simply weak because charts look heavy. It is weak because the macro message got less friendly: inflation data remained hot, GDP looked strong enough to deny near-term relief, oil may be trying to form a major low, and the dollar still has room to transmit pressure into commodities, crypto, emerging-market risk, high beta, and duration-heavy technology.

The working posture is defensive confirmation, not blind crash certainty. The tape can still generate sharp countertrend bounces, especially if chip/memory names squeeze or the dollar pauses. But the burden of proof has shifted. Mega-cap weakness, discretionary breakdown, crypto pressure, metals still waiting for final washout, and oil-bottom risk together argue that rallies should be treated as tactical until breadth and funding channels confirm relief.
