Risk Notes and Open Questions

  • Regime: A falling dollar with rising oil and long yields is inflationary instability, not clean easing.
  • Oil extremes: 200-220 is a stress branch, not a base forecast. Confirm it through 119.41, persistent backwardation or supply stress, inflation expectations, and sector breadth.
  • Policy: Do not assume long-end support is free liquidity. A policy action that lowers yields while weakening the dollar can extend nominal assets and worsen the next inflation impulse.
  • Leadership: AI hardware resilience does not offset broad deterioration unless software, consumer, small caps, and credit-sensitive groups join.
  • Crypto: Use speculative sizing. Three-wave rebounds, protocol risk, custody risk, and future cryptographic threats remain separate from the chart thesis.
