Today's Operating Read

Operating posture: selective participation with explicit failure conditions. Some earlier bearish branches have been invalidated by higher highs. Other apparent bargains still require a reclaim or a completed corrective structure. Neither an optimistic AI investment story nor a dramatic downside scenario replaces a stop, position-size decision, or observed confirmation.

Research Rules

  1. Score invalidation honestly. The SMH and MSFT bearish branches lost support after new highs. Record that change before proposing a new forecast.
  2. Separate rebound from regime repair. A tactical bounce needs price support; a broader upgrade also needs participation and less financing pressure. Test these independently over the next five sessions.
  3. Preserve failed-trade history. An ETHUSD stop breach remains a failed setup even if a later entry works. Do not widen the old stop retroactively.
  4. Make reclaim entries executable. Z above 29 is not the same rule as buying every print below the watchlist entry. A level, activation condition, and invalidation must belong to the same setup.
  5. Test rotation across instruments. XLU/XLP strength with weak small caps is defensive rotation; strength spreading into IWM, banks, and housing with firmer bonds is stronger evidence of repair.
  6. Keep explanations falsifiable. Stop-run or manipulation stories are not proof. Observe whether the specified level held, whether the entry was available, and whether the anticipated move occurred.

Bottom Line

Relief has returned, and some bearish leadership calls have been invalidated. The market still has unresolved pressure in rates, breadth, and cyclical groups. Keep the improving structures, retire explicitly cancelled fresh-entry ideas, and let next week's inflation and employment response determine whether this becomes broader repair or another rally inside a correction.
