Regime Map

The market is prioritizing cash flow, profitability, and balance-sheet durability. That is a different regime from the speculative-growth tape. Companies selling distant future revenue without present execution are now being repriced harder because oil, rates, and inflation pressure raise the cost of waiting.

This was not a full crash day because semiconductors and some internal pockets still held up better than the headline weakness implied. That makes the next decision point cleaner: if SMH and EWY fail from here, the market can move from leadership damage into index-level liquidation. If they recover, QQQ can still build one final rally after a controlled low.
