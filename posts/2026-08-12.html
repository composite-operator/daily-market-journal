Research Rules Learned

  • Classify inflation from the joint response in the dollar, rates, oil, volatility, and breadth. An in-line headline is not a regime verdict.
  • When indexes disagree, use relative structure: IWM is the bullish test, DIA is the bearish warning, and SPY and QQQ are the contested middle.
  • Require semiconductor components to confirm the sector. A narrow equipment rally does not prove durable technology breadth.
  • Let price contradict the narrative. Copper above 7 would require a new interpretation even if industrial and currency relationships appear inconsistent.
  • Separate a reaction from a durable low. Bitcoin near 39,000, metals at deeper boxes, and high-beta connector lows still require follow-through.
  • Preserve the imported board when speech and tracking list disagree. Put unresolved closures, removals, and duplicate rows in Needs Review.
  • Use normal position sizing for low-priced, volatile securities. A low nominal price does not reduce setup risk.
