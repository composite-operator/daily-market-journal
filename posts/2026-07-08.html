Regime Map

The market had a late relief rally because investors treated the geopolitical path as contained. That relief did not repair the larger flow map. Oil has a confirmed breakout with a major pivot, the dollar is trying to enter a stronger impulse, long-end yields erased divergence and squeezed higher, and VIX is no longer asleep. Those channels tighten liquidity even when tech squeezes intraday.

Small caps are the clearest warning. The Russell stopped calling the bond market a liar and started following higher rates lower. IWM is now in a bearish daily-cycle correction with downside references around 271 and 267/270. The rate-sensitive crowd positioned too early for lower rates; if yields push further, those trades can unwind and create better long opportunities later.

Technology is not clean leadership. QQQ likely invalidated the triangle and is now treated as a more impulsive downside structure, with a possible wave-two rally as high as the 728 area before another lower move. AI hardware and hyperscalers remain weak together; a rotation from memory into platforms is not enough if the headline index keeps losing breadth.
