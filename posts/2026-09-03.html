Macro Flow Tape

Relief channel: Softer dollar and rate pressure gave risk assets room to rally. The important evidence is the price response, not any single policy explanation. A durable improvement requires the dollar, long bonds, the yen, volatility, and equity breadth to continue moving toward easier conditions together.

Late-cycle breadth: The broader market trend can remain higher while leadership becomes selective. Small caps now have a defined lower reaction area, but the current rally still needs confirmation from semiconductors and weaker megacap structures.

Oil and inflation: Oil can complete another fifth-wave push and then correct. If crude instead accelerates, it can reverse the relief in rates, margins, and consumers. The market therefore has a narrow window in which risk can extend before energy becomes restrictive again.

Dollar-yen-volatility cluster: The more dangerous combination is a stronger dollar, weaker yen, weaker long bonds, and rising volatility. That alignment would transmit stress through commodities, crypto, high beta, emerging markets, and industrial cyclicals at the same time. It remains a confirmation test, not a completed regime shift.

Crypto and speculative liquidity: Bitcoin's improved structure and HOOD breakout weaken the immediate liquidation thesis. They do not remove the possibility of another large corrective swing. Use the next three-wave pullback and the reaction in crypto-sensitive equities to judge whether speculative liquidity is expanding or merely squeezing.
