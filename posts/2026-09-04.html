Macro Flow Tape

Inflation-week fork: PPI, CPI, and the FOMC meeting form the next policy sequence. Cooler inflation supports lower yields, a weaker dollar, housing, regional banks, small caps, crypto, and long-duration technology. Hot inflation raises the probability of tighter policy and reverses that chain.

Oil as the transmission channel: Crude is no longer only an energy trade. Sustained strength raises transport costs, inflation expectations, long yields, and pressure on consumer margins. A completed oil advance followed by a controlled correction would give the risk rally more room.

AI concentration versus physical investment: Expectations for leading AI companies remain difficult to satisfy even when demand is strong. The broader opportunity map is shifting toward the equipment and infrastructure required to support electrification, energy production, robotics, grid capacity, materials, and health care.

Regional banks as a regime tell: Improving regional-bank structures challenge the most bearish credit branch. Durable strength would fit lower long rates, better loan demand, and broader small-cap participation. A failed pullback would preserve the defensive bank and real-estate interpretation.

Dollar and carry: A dollar breakdown with a stable yen would support international and commodity breadth. A stronger dollar beside a weaker yen would tighten global liquidity and raise the risk of synchronized selling.
