Regime Map

The market is pricing a benign path: geopolitical de-escalation, weaker energy prices, lower inflation, and easier monetary policy. That expectation supported the Dow, health care, staples, food, software, and selected industrials. The cross-asset evidence does not yet confirm the story. Oil is near support, the dollar is rising, long bonds remain weak, and semiconductors still show incomplete downside.

The next policy decision and major technology earnings can overwhelm the current rotation. A surprise tightening outcome is only a rumor, not a base fact, but the market is vulnerable because small caps, metals, and duration-sensitive assets are positioned for easier conditions. The valid stance is conditional: follow confirmed price and cross-asset behavior after the event.
