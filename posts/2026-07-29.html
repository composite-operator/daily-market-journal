Regime Rules Learned

  • Call an unusual cross-asset move a regime signal only after it persists beyond one event session.
  • Separate policy uncertainty from proven capital flight. Confirm with the dollar, long bonds, volatility, credit, and international relative strength.
  • Do not treat every buy-box touch as equal. Wide boxes require smaller size or reversal evidence.
  • When a strong bounce fails and corrects the prior decline, tighten risk because the next leg can extend sharply.
  • Require semiconductor breadth to stabilize before treating QQQ support as durable.
  • Use divergence as a state variable. Preserved divergence allows a late-wave turn; erased divergence favors extension.
  • Keep official tracking list levels separate from preferred sub-areas spoken during chart review.
