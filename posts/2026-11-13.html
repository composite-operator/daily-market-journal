  • Test the final-low claim: over the next one, three, and five trading sessions, record whether SPY, QQQ, SMH, and NVDA make lower price lows with weaker downside momentum, then reclaim support. Accelerating momentum and failed reclaims count against the claim.
  • Separate branch from order: a deep downside scenario is not a limit buy. A chart-only idea needs an exact entry, invalidation, timeframe, and condition before promotion into numeric tracking fields.
  • Audit breadth: compare equal-weight versus capitalization-weighted equities, small caps, semiconductors, and credit. A rebound confined to a few large names is not broad repair.
  • Keep execution evidence separate: model-filled commentary is not a brokerage fill. Do not infer a sale from disappearance or calculate realized performance from an assumed exit.
  • Do not backfill confidence: score the original trigger, stop, and horizon before evaluating a revised wave count. Missing market observations remain unscored.
