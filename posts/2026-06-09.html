Trade Management Rules

  • Do not buy large daily boxes until divergence is erased. This rule applies especially to metals, miners, materials, uranium, and other large box structures.
  • A box fill without reaction is information. Failed reactions in TAN, solar, energy, or small-cap boxes should not be given extra room simply because the original setup looked attractive.
  • Second-chance entries stay separate from original entries. Tonight produced more "wait for another pullback" language than clean new second-chance levels.
  • Long-side wins should be smaller and faster in this regime. TSLA was explicitly framed as a small scalp / risk-management trade, not a risk-on green light.
  • Equity prices in snapshots reflect the 4pm closing price. Futures and crypto prices may reflect the true price at the time of the snapshot.

Regime Rules Learned
