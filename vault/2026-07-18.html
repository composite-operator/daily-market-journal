Regime Map

The market has shifted from broad soft-landing relief into a valuation-discipline regime. CPI and PPI cooled enough to spark rate-sensitive optimism, but inflation is still above target and oil is rising into geopolitical escalation. The read does not accept the easy "summer of disinflation" story unless the dollar breaks, rates relax, oil stops pressing, and gold/silver ratio behavior confirms.

AI is no longer treated as an infinite-capital narrative. Hyperscaler CDS spreads are widening, Oracle is flagged as the canary because of debt-funded AI infrastructure and weak free-cash-flow trajectory, and even Nvidia credit insurance deserves monitoring. The central question is whether AI infrastructure spend produces enough cash return to justify the capital stack.

The week closed with semiconductors and thematic Mag7 vehicles breaking down while energy, real estate, and financials led. That rotation is not automatically healthy. Energy leadership fits the inflation/geopolitical channel. Real estate strength depends on lower rates and can be rug-pulled if rates rise again. Banks had strong earnings, but the read treats them as late-cycle fifth-wave optimism rather than confirmed new leadership.
