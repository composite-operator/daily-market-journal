2026-07-13

Oil, CPI, And Semis Put Risk On Trial

  • Do not upgrade a relief rally unless the macro channels agree: dollar , rates , volatility , breadth, and leadership must confirm together.
  • Separate hard-asset strength from broad risk appetite. Energy can be bullish for energy while bearish for consumer margins, inflation expectations, and equity duration.
  • Use semiconductors and AI-hardware breadth as the stress test for headline-index strength.
  • When a crowded sleeve breaks, assume deleveraging can override good single-name stories.
  • Treat CPI as a catalyst, not a complete regime answer. The follow-through across dollar , rates , oil , and volatility is the real signal.
  • Build shopping lists during crash-correction windows, but wait for support zones, divergence resets, and confirmation before treating them as durable entries.

2026-07-11

Commodity Signals Split The Macro Tape

  • Treat cross-asset agreement as stronger evidence than any single index pattern.
  • When relief rallies arrive, require volatility , dollar , breadth, and leadership to confirm before upgrading regime posture.
  • Technology leadership should be tested against breadth; narrow leadership is less durable when cyclicals and small caps lag.
  • Commodity strength should be separated into inflation impulse, demand strength, and liquidity hedge before assigning regime meaning.
  • Credit-sensitive groups should receive extra weight when judging whether equity weakness is ordinary rotation or liquidity stress.
  • Mixed tapes should be traded as confirmation problems: wait for one side of the capital-flow stack to resolve.

  • Late technology relief is low-quality evidence when semiconductors , small caps , and volatility disagree.
  • Yen carry risk should be monitored in stages: the first move warns, the second unwind can break market structure.
  • Copper and industrial-resource strength can diverge from precious metals when the market is pricing infrastructure and capacity expansion.
  • Visual-only chart levels should stay in review status until a numeric level is confirmed.
