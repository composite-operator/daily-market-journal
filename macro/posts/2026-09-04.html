Macro Flow Tape

Disinflation extension: PPI and CPI cool, the dollar weakens, long bonds recover, and oil pauses. Regional banks, housing, small caps, crypto, and duration assets broaden the advance.

Inflation reset: Oil and inflation data surprise higher, long yields rise, and the dollar firms. Rate-sensitive growth, housing, small caps, and consumer exposure lose support together.

Physical-investment rotation: Grid, energy equipment, materials, robotics, and medical infrastructure hold pullbacks while capital becomes more selective inside headline technology.

Narrow blow-off: Semiconductors and megacap indexes make another high without regional-bank, small-cap, or cyclical confirmation. The advance extends, but its concentration increases the consequence of failure.

Funding squeeze: The dollar and yields rise while the yen weakens and volatility expands. International risk, commodities, crypto, and high beta then contract together.

Operating Conditions

  • Upgrade risk: Inflation cools, oil pauses, long bonds recover, and breadth expands through small caps and regional banks.
  • Downgrade risk: Oil, yields, the dollar, and volatility rise together after the inflation releases.
  • Confirm physical investment: Grid, materials, oil services, batteries, and robotics hold corrective pullbacks with improving component breadth.
  • Reject a clean all-clear: A flat index week and lower volatility do not resolve the policy and funding channels.
  • Confirm speculative liquidity: Bitcoin and crypto-sensitive equities hold their next pullback while the dollar remains weak.
  • Confirm concentration risk: Semiconductors or megacaps rise while equal-weight technology, banks, and cyclicals fail to participate.

Research Rules

  • Separate a flat weekly index result from a stable regime. Rates, currencies, oil, and breadth can change materially beneath an unchanged index.
  • Verify scheduled catalysts with official calendars before assigning timing to a macro branch.
  • Use oil as both an asset and a transmission channel through inflation, yields, transport, consumers, and margins.
  • Require regional-bank breadth to confirm the lower-rate and small-cap expansion thesis.
  • Keep systematic-screen candidates in a research sleeve until price supplies a defined correction, entry, stop, and invalidation.
  • Distinguish multi-year thematic quality from current trade location. A strong secular theme can still be a poor immediate entry.
  • Use component breadth to test an ETF thesis. Grid, oil services, medical devices, and robotics must not depend on one constituent.
  • Do not convert a possible future reaction area into a current official level.
  • Require the dollar, long bonds, the yen, volatility, and oil to confirm the same liquidity direction before declaring a regime change.
  • Preserve alternative counts when the evidence is structurally ambiguous, but attach each branch to observable confirmation and failure conditions.

Bottom Line

The market enters inflation week with a wider set of possible capital destinations and an unresolved funding regime. Cooler inflation, contained crude, lower yields, and a weaker dollar would support regional banks, housing, small caps, crypto, and another technology extension. Hot inflation or renewed oil acceleration would restore tightening and expose the week's flat index result as false calm. The broad opportunity map is useful, but only corrective location and cross-asset confirmation can turn it into an expansion regime.
