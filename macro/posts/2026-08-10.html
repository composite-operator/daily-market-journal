Regime Map

The regime is shifting from narrow late-cycle extension toward a confirmation test. Energy is strengthening, industrial leadership is fragile, and semiconductor support has failed. This combination can produce stagflationary pressure: nominal commodity strength alongside weaker growth-sensitive breadth.

The next inflation release is the main catalyst, but the release itself is not the signal. The signal is the joint reaction in oil, the dollar, long-end rates, semiconductors, volatility, and small caps. A cool reading that lowers rates and the dollar can restore risk appetite. A hot reading while oil is already advancing can reactivate tightening expectations and accelerate the semiconductor break.

Small caps are the directional tie-breaker. Their corrective structure can still resolve into a strong extension, but failure from the current pivot would align them with semiconductors and confirm broader capital retreat.

Mixed branch: Oil rises while the dollar softens. Commodities and nominal asset prices can remain firm, but the move is inflationary rather than cleanly expansionary. Energy and defense can lead while consumer and duration-heavy assets lose relative strength.

Research Rules

  • Use the joint reaction to an economic release, not the headline number alone, to classify the regime.
  • Require semiconductor component confirmation before promoting an index rebound into a durable impulse.
  • Distinguish commodity leadership from healthy breadth. Energy can rise while the rest of the economy tightens.
  • Translate geopolitical narratives into observable oil, dollar, rate, volatility, and breadth tests.
  • Evaluate AI theses through asset ownership, marginal cost, execution, and demand. Do not let a technology story override chart controls.
  • Treat distant targets as scenarios. Publish intermediate levels, failure points, and the expected corrective path.
