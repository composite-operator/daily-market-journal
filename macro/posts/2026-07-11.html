Sector Transmission Map

The sector map matters more than isolated names. The key is whether leadership expands across risk sleeves or keeps narrowing into a few defensive or crowded pockets.

Tactical Confirmation

Upgrade the tape only if rallies become impulsive, breadth expands, volatility fades, and dollar pressure stops spreading across assets. Downgrade the tape if rallies continue to arrive as weak bounces into resistance while volatility and defensive flow stay firm.

This is an evidence-first regime read: support tests can matter, but the macro lens asks whether capital is actually moving into risk or merely pausing before the next liquidity test.
