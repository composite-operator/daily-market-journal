Macro Flow Tape

The first signal is synchronized relief. Oil, rates, and the dollar moved in a direction that reduces immediate pressure on risk assets. This can extend the rally even while the larger regime remains unsettled.

The second signal is incomplete leadership. Semiconductor weakness prevents the index move from qualifying as broad capital expansion. The rally is stronger than a simple bounce, but not yet strong enough to close the confirmation gap.

The third signal is a two-timeframe market. Tactical longs can work while the higher-timeframe tightening branch remains active. Explicit risk controls allow participation without converting temporary relief into unconditional conviction.
