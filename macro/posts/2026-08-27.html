Research Rules

  • Use the dollar, oil, and long yields together to distinguish durable index direction from intraday positioning noise.
  • An earnings catalyst is not durable sector leadership until adjacent names and the sector ETF confirm the move.
  • Volatility compression before a binary policy event increases the speed of the losing side's forced repositioning.
  • Software leadership should be scored by earnings quality and impulse persistence, not by indiscriminate group performance.
  • Commodity strength alongside rising yields is a reflation warning, not evidence of clean monetary easing.
  • Negative small-cap and consumer participation beneath megacap strength is evidence of narrow transmission.
  • When higher-degree corrections dominate the tape, reduce turnover and require defined risk areas aligned with the larger cycle.
  • Do not convert a spoken status conflict or ambiguous ticker into a ledger mutation without authoritative confirmation.

Bottom Line

AI and software can carry the market higher, but the rally remains conditional on the policy and rates channel. A softer dollar with lower yields would broaden relief; a simultaneous rise in the dollar, oil, and long yields would expose an underhedged crowd and reassert the restrictive regime. Treat Jackson Hole as a catalyst, not an explanation. The explanation must come from cross-asset transmission and breadth after the event.
