Research Rules

  • Separate index concentration from market breadth. A technology-led decline is not broad liquidation until adjacent groups confirm it.
  • Use a 48-hour follow-through test after a leadership shock. Immediate continuation carries more regime information than later choppy weakness.
  • Score oil strength twice: once as sector leadership and once as an inflation tax transmitted through long rates.
  • Require dollar, yen, rates, and volatility agreement before labeling a global liquidity contraction.
  • Treat the gold-silver ratio as a quality check on commodity breadth. Above 66 favors defensive metal leadership; below it supports broader reflation.
  • Do not turn chart-visible levels into numeric rules unless the level and invalidation are both confirmed in the record.
  • Distinguish tactical bounces from durable lows. A support reaction without breadth and leadership repair is only a tradeable counter-move.

Bottom Line

The tape is testing whether concentrated AI leadership can correct without taking the wider market with it. The long-rate and oil backdrop makes that test less forgiving, but breadth has not yet confirmed generalized liquidation. The decisive combination is semiconductor follow-through, long yields, USDJPY near 160.90, and volatility. Agreement among those channels would mark a defensive regime shift; divergence among them would keep the move in the category of a sharp but repairable leadership reset.
