Research Rules

  • Separate a softer pressure impulse from a durable easing regime. One favorable session does not establish persistent liquidity expansion.
  • Require breadth beneath the indexes. Small caps and semiconductor components must confirm headline strength.
  • Use the dollar, long bonds, the yen, volatility, and oil as a joint confirmation cluster. Do not promote one channel into a regime verdict by itself.
  • Distinguish an invalidated support zone from an invalidated long-term thesis. Remove stale execution levels and wait for exact replacements.
  • Keep official entries, fills, and stops separate from rounded discussion areas and short-horizon trader support zones.
  • When an active-trade control breaks and the position is described as out, close the trade rather than preserve it under a generic review label.
  • Treat speculative-liquidity improvement as provisional until the next pullback holds.
  • Use Australia and industrial breadth to test the copper and global-growth thesis.
  • Reject causal stories that lack confirmation from price, breadth, currencies, rates, credit, volatility, and observable supply conditions.
  • Prefer explicit uncertainty over false precision when an asset handoff is unclear. A probable action belongs in a separate queue from confirmed actions.
