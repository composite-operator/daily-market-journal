Regime Map

The defensive branch had the stronger pre-event evidence. Research Rules

  • Classify an economic release from the joint market reaction, not from the headline number or a predicted policy response.
  • Do not infer economic prosperity from commodity inflation. Trade direct composition and price relationships instead of narrative correlations.
  • Require components to confirm sector indexes. An ETF breakout without component participation remains suspect.
  • Distinguish a short-term catalyst from the long-term route. A relief rally can coexist with a mature higher-timeframe cycle.
  • Use crowded divergence and failed cycles as risk evidence, not automatic timing signals.
  • Keep opposing branches until their stated control levels resolve. Do not promote a preferred narrative into certainty.
