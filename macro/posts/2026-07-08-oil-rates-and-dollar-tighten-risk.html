Macro Flow Tape

The tape is not simply risk-off. It is a sequence problem. Oil and rates are tightening first. That can force a shakeout in small caps, housing, real estate, banks, healthcare, and other lower-rate beneficiaries before those same areas become attractive later. The macro thesis can be correct while the trade is early.

Technology is also failing the quality test. Late-session bounces and narrative pushes can create short-term relief, but they have not repaired breadth. If QQQ, SMH, and IWM continue to weaken while mega-cap relief attempts keep fading, the market is showing distribution rather than leadership rotation.

The dollar is the main cross-asset gate. With DXY breaking higher, metals and crypto remain vulnerable even if their own charts briefly bounce. Precious metals need the dollar and the gold/silver ratio to line up. Crypto needs basing and trapped-supply repair. China and EM need dollar relief.

Energy is the opposite side of the flow. A crude breakout can be bullish for energy and agriculture/capacity themes while simultaneously bearish for broad risk through inflation, rates, and overnight geopolitical risk. That is the current split: selected resource exposure improving while liquidity-sensitive risk remains under pressure.
