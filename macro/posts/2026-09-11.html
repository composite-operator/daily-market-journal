Scenario Matrix

Orderly relief: Oil consolidates below its next acceleration zone, long yields reverse, volatility remains contained, and the Fed communication does not strengthen the dollar sharply. Small caps, software, housing, and banks can catch up. This becomes durable repair only if breadth persists.

Grinding stagflation: Oil remains elevated, core inflation continues to ease slowly, and long yields stay restrictive. Energy and infrastructure cash flows outperform while consumer and duration exposures weaken through rotation rather than immediate collapse.

Policy-shock liquidation: The Fed signals a sustained hiking path, the long end continues higher, and volatility breaks out. Metals, crypto, housing, software, and crowded growth become vulnerable together. A stronger yen would intensify the funding stress.

Inflationary rescue: Policy or Treasury action suppresses yields without resolving oil supply. Risk assets can rally first, but dollar weakness, commodities, and inflation expectations become the failure gauges. The relief phase can worsen the next inflation problem.

Supply normalization: Alternative routes and restored production gradually rebuild inventories. Oil retreats, headline inflation cools, and policy flexibility returns. This is the cleanest constructive branch, but it requires observable flows and inventory repair rather than headlines.

Research Rules

  1. Separate headline inflation from core inflation. Identify the source before assigning a growth, policy, or sector conclusion.
  2. Read the joint move. Oil, long yields, the dollar, the yen, volatility, credit, and breadth classify the regime together.
  3. Separate relief from repair. A rally can occur inside tightening financial conditions.
  4. Test the transmission. A policy thesis is not confirmed until rates, currencies, volatility, credit, and leadership respond as expected.
  5. Separate secular demand from entry quality. AI infrastructure scarcity can be a strong multi-year thesis and a poor near-term trade at the same time.
  6. Use explicit horizons. A Fed-week trade, a six-month correction, and a multi-year capital cycle need different evidence and invalidations.
  7. Write the falsifier first. Lower oil, lower yields, contained volatility, and broader participation weaken the defensive case; the opposite combination strengthens it.

Bottom Line

The market is not yet in confirmed liquidation, but the policy exit has narrowed. Friday's relief rally can extend while volatility remains contained. Durable relief requires the energy shock to stop accelerating and the long end to turn lower without a disorderly currency move. If oil and yields rise together after the Fed while breadth remains narrow, the tape moves from unstable relief into a stronger defensive regime.
