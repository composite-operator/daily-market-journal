2026-09-02: The market produced a narrow relief rally when oil and long yields stopped accelerating. Nvidia and Dell supported the headline indexes, while sharp losses in several adjacent technology names showed that capital remained selective. The regime has not returned to broad risk-on. The bearish alignment is conditional rather than complete. It requires crude to accelerate, the dollar to strengthen, long bonds to fail, the yen to weaken, and volatility to reverse higher together. If those markets instead move toward easier conditions, SPY and QQQ can extend the relief phase. A rebound alone does not settle the larger correction.

Research Rules

  • Separate stabilization from reversal. A slower rise in oil or yields is not the same as easier financial conditions.
  • Measure breadth beneath headline indexes before describing an AI-led rally as durable.
  • Require the dollar, yen, long bonds, volatility, and crude to confirm the same risk direction before declaring a regime shift.
  • Use support areas as conditional reaction zones, not promises that a durable low is in place.
  • Keep exact ledger entries and stops separate from rounded research levels.
  • When a stated stop update conflicts with the ledger, preserve the ledger, label Needs Review, and block automation.
  • Test geopolitical explanations with official reporting, supply data, shipping costs, insurance stress, and observable price transmission.
  • Use Australian equities and industrial breadth to challenge or confirm the secular copper thesis.
  • Do not use political timing, intervention stories, or prior pattern similarity as proof of cause or outcome.
  • Distinguish a tactical hedge from a directional forecast. A valid hedge can lose money without invalidating the risk-control process.

Bottom Line

The market has moved from immediate tightening pressure into a fragile relief phase. That phase can extend if oil cools, long bonds recover, the dollar weakens, and semiconductor breadth improves. It fails if crude, the dollar, yields, and volatility align while technology participation narrows further. The evidence supports conditional defense, not crash certainty and not a restored risk-on regime. Let the cross-asset cluster determine which path is becoming real.
