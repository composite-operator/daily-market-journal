Macro Flow Tape

The main change is that index risk now has a clearer warning from the Dow. A failed cycle does not guarantee a crash, but it raises the burden of proof for bulls. If SPY and QQQ hold the cliff and squeeze, that rally should still be judged against breadth, volatility, and the dollar. If they fail, the market can move quickly because compression is already built into the structure.

Crypto is the opposite side of the tape. Bitcoin follow-through, Ethereum cycle-low discussion, Solana conditions, and XRP's wedge breakout all point to a possible reflation impulse. That matters because crypto often senses liquidity before slower equity sectors do. But crypto strength is not enough by itself. If the dollar rises and semiconductors break, crypto can become a narrow pocket rather than a broad risk-on signal.

Oil is the most important cross-current. A durable oil breakout can make energy and selected resource names work, but it also keeps inflation and rate pressure alive. That split is why the market can be bullish in energy and defensive in broad indices at the same time. The right macro read is not "risk on" or "risk off"; it is segmented capital flow.

Software and old-economy weakness deserve respect. Sharp breaks in large, previously stable names can be early evidence that capital is leaving crowded safety or late-cycle growth before the index rolls over. When that weakness appears beside a fragile Dow and vulnerable semiconductors, leadership quality is deteriorating even if the tape has not fully broken.
