Second, can metals and miners establish controlled pullback entries while the dollar remains firm? Third, does the emerging strength in agriculture reflect measurable supply pressure or only an early price narrative? The answers must come from cross-asset transmission, not a forecast headline.

Research Rules

  • Separate observed market response from the policy forecast used to explain it.
  • A regime claim requires propagation through multiple independent channels, not one asset or headline.
  • Treat cross-market non-confirmation as evidence. Real estate and regional-bank resilience currently weaken the strongest tightening thesis.
  • Use an event reaction to define pivots and invalidation. Do not treat the first reaction as a complete long-cycle verdict.
  • Weather, crop-failure, food-shortage, reserve, and shipping claims require independent data before they enter the core thesis.
  • Historical concentration analogies explain mechanics; they do not prove timing or magnitude.
